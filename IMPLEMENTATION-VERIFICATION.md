## Implemented behavior

- **Physical economy:** versioned cargo rules, finite stock, atomic pickup reservations, carried loads, friendly delivery, cancellation, crash recovery/loss and conservation. Shared income is earned on delivery. The simplified shop includes cargo, gun, optics, battery, separate armor and paid timed rearm. Refitting cannot manufacture ammunition or charge. Historical cube/miner/jammer recordings retain their meaning.
- **Scenery:** marked matte crates and pallets, visibly shrinking/empty stock, attached cargo, three marked service positions, team bases and body colors, and clearer city materials. The central depot is accessible through actual collision geometry, with greater exposure. Its blue/red route lengths are unequal; no balance or symmetry claim is made.
- **Own sensing and control:** calibrated units/axes, velocity and native MAVLink gimbal telemetry, true downward camera aim, finite anonymous proximity/downward readings, measured freshness, physical local braking and caller-selected travel/precision profiles. Camera acquisition pose remains distinct from current telemetry. The controller chooses no route or tactical destination.
- **Jobs and model feedback:** ordered routes, explicit single-writer replacement, locally renewed leases, lifecycle cancellation, event/observation provenance, and up to eight compatible submissions with per-operation results and one fresh aggregate camera/inbox bundle. Completed side effects are not rolled back by later rejection. Ordinary chat preserves the objective.
- **Private programming:** bounded atomic workspaces and retained source versions, measured deployed QuickJS assets, terminable workers, native thread CPU accounting, finite heap/call/deadline limits and a narrow own-state SDK. Imported code is inert until the recipient explicitly imports and starts it. No solved gameplay routines or optional vision libraries are preinstalled.
- **Native communication:** isolated team Zenoh networks, durable bounded mail, backpressure before receipt, monotonic expiry, deduplication, latest-status ordering, actual storage/bundle/reply stages, bounded inert file transfers and ordinary blue-player chat/replies. Red cannot access the blue player. Native failures remain explicit.
- **Watchability:** live cargo/service/job/storage displays and bounded replay evidence for script versions, SDK execution, cancellation and radio delivery. Historical source is displayed inertly and is inaccessible to later actors.

The public interface and storage/lifecycle boundaries are documented in [ONBOARD.md](ONBOARD.md), [ONBOARD-RUNTIME.md](ONBOARD-RUNTIME.md), [NETWORK.md](NETWORK.md) and [MAVLINK.md](MAVLINK.md).
