### P2 — Logistics never starts, leaving the blue gun without ammunition

Both teams spent their opening 30 on a gun: Red 2 at sim 25.983 and Blue 2 at 26.991. There were **no loading or delivery events**, no additional equipment purchases and no rearming. Blue 2 exhausted its magazine at 467.443, leaving roughly 110 simulation seconds with no rounds or credits to buy the 10-credit rearm service. Red 2 retained 11 rounds at the end.

Salvage was visible in at least one inspected acquired image: Blue 3's sim 172.288 frame shows yellow crates and a marked loading apron at lower left. Its subsequent radio report discusses an uncertain airframe sighting. Visible cargo does not establish that the pilot recognized or intentionally ignored it, but this rules out a blanket claim that no salvage ever entered a pilot camera.

Evidence: `haul-analysis.json`, `analysis.json`, and `reviewed-camera-evidence.json`. Camera: `1bc1a4a4-b955-4eac-9e4b-0bdb7384993b.jpg` under the run's replay directory.

**Next check:** run the existing single-haul scenario, repeat it, then run team hauling on this exact source. Evaluate recognition and physical approach separately. This normal battle does not close those gates or establish an economy-mechanics defect.

### P2 — The focused trial exposes cockpit links without a cockpit API

Opening Blue 2's cockpit during the battle produced **“Unexpected token '<' … is not valid JSON”**, `RECONNECTING`, and empty evidence cards. `GET /api/cockpit/drone-2` on 4319 returned HTTP 200 with HTML. The trial host mounts state, diagnostics and replay endpoints but omits the cockpit store/router; Vite handles the unmatched request as an application page.

The current production server on 4317 returned the expected `fleet-cockpit/1` JSON. The older idle service on 4318 also returned HTML; its source was not changed or retested. This finding specifically reproduces the missing integration in [scripts/trial-host.ts](scripts/trial-host.ts), rather than establishing a failure in the current production cockpit.

Evidence: `cockpit-error.png`, `cockpit-error.txt`, `cockpit-http-evidence.json`; compare [server/index.ts](server/index.ts). Camera acquisition continued while the cockpit was open. **Suggested repair:** wire the production cockpit recording/router into the trial host and verify live observation, output and workspace inspection there.

### P2 — A destroyed airframe remains visible and prompts an extra shot

Red 3 died at sim 315.318. Blue 2's acquired frame at 318.137 still shows a dark, tilted airframe at the same location. At 01:15:45.759 UTC, its emitted summary describes the color change as possible armor loss and plans another shot at the black target. The shot at 325.494 expires without a hit.

[client/drone-model.ts](client/drone-model.ts) keeps the mesh at the recorded position, tilts it and darkens its body when dead. The observed pilot confused this visual state with a surviving target. This is one supported readability failure, not evidence that every missed shot targeted a wreck.

Evidence: `ebd8dec6-1a8d-41ba-bd79-7f95c39889fb.jpg`, the adjacent native summary records and the fourth shot in `analysis.json`. **Suggested check:** compare unmistakable visible destruction treatment in acquired-camera fixtures, then validate it with a live encounter. Preserve pixel-based recognition and generic local hit feedback.
